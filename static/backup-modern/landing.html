01 / PROBLEM

Creating Digital Surface Models traditionally requires LiDAR surveys, stereo satellite pairs, or drone photogrammetry — expensive, multi-pass operations that demand specialized hardware and significant compute.

02 / APPROACH

DepthWizard estimates metric elevation from a single optical image. A fine-tuned monocular depth model, calibrated with RANSAC regression and optionally refined with ground control points, produces GIS-ready GeoTIFF output — with honest validation numbers.
